1. WHY DID IT WORK PREVIOUSLY WITHOUT ANY ENTRIES?
================================================================================
If your Gmail, Stripe, and Slack had no entries yet, but the investigation 
completed with full evidence, here is why:

1. Preset Demo Scenarios (01 to 06):
   - In the dashboard, the 6 quick-demo buttons ("01 True Duplicate", 
     "02 False Duplicate", etc.) attach a `scenario_id` (e.g., 
     `scenario_1_true_duplicate`).
   - RECON is architected so that any request with a preset `scenario_id` 
     routes to the deterministic mock adapters. 
   - This ensures hackathon judges and evaluators can test and benchmark the 
     system immediately, with 100% deterministic reproducibility, even before 
     connecting live credentials.

2. Fallback & Graceful Degradation:
   - If an external integration is unreachable or returns 0 records, the 
     reconciliation engine does NOT crash. It detects 0 records, flags it as 
     a risk, and escalates to human review ("ESCALATE_FOR_REVIEW").


================================================================================
2. HOW TO TRIGGER 100% TRUE [LIVE] MODE
================================================================================
To test with your REAL accounts instead of the preset scenario fixtures:

1. Check your .env file in the project root:
   MOCK_MODE=false

2. In the RECON Web UI (http://localhost:5173):
   - Go to the "Investigation" tab.
   - In the "Investigation Query" box:
     * Enter the customer name you created in Stripe/Gmail.
     * Enter the exact customer email address.
     * Type a natural language query (e.g., "Investigate double billing for Alex").
   - DO NOT click the preset scenario buttons (01-06), as those reload the 
     mock scenario fixtures.
   - Click [ Investigate ].
   - RECON will now connect directly to your live Gmail inbox, query your Stripe 
     Test ledger, and read your Slack #billing channel!

5. SLACK ENTRY (INTERNAL CONTEXT IN #billing)
================================================================================
Open your Slack workspace where your RECON Bot is installed.

Step 5.1: Channel Configuration
- Ensure a public channel named `#billing` exists (or create one: `#billing`).
- Invite your bot to the channel by typing inside `#billing`:
  /invite @RECON
  (replace @RECON with your bot's app name).

Step 5.2: Post Internal Context Message
As any user in the `#billing` channel, post this message:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hey team, regarding Acme Corp (alex@acmecorp.com): their signup webhook had a 
network timeout yesterday and retried, creating an accidental duplicate 
charge of $499. They only have one active subscription. If they reach out, 
please refund the second charge.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Why this matches RECON's reconciliation engine:
- RECON's Slack adapter searches `#billing` for the customer name/email and 
  billing keywords.
- Phrases like "accidental", "only have one", and "please refund" corroborate 
  the customer's claim that only 1 charge was expected.
- Result: System verifies that the dispute is a TRUE duplicate, not a legitimate 
  second fee.

Post-Execution Verification:
- When you click [ Approve refund ] in RECON, the safety engine validates the 
  action, executes the refund via Stripe API, and automatically posts a verified 
  delivery receipt directly to `#billing`!
